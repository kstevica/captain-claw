# Welcome to your research desk

Commission research the way you'd brief a human team — in plain language,
with the scope, geography and time frame you care about.

What happens next:

1. **A plan, before any spend.** Your team drafts a coordination plan — who
   researches what, in which order. You review and approve it (or cancel and
   rephrase) before the run starts.
2. **A team works the brief.** Specialist agents research in parallel, share
   a workspace, and hand their findings to a reporter.
3. **A report with receipts.** Claims are checked, key values land in a facts
   ledger, and every report carries its own verification trail and cost line.
4. **Streams deepen.** Follow-up rounds build on the same workspace — ask for
   more depth, a revision, or the gaps filled, and the team continues where it
   left off.
